Warehouse Picking & Packing

Calipokehouse Training · one short cartoon per step, then a quick test

How to use: pick a lesson, press Play, and the scenes advance on their own as the narration finishes. Use ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys to step through, Space to pause. Narration uses the browser's built-in voice; turn it off to read the subtitles at your own pace. Each lesson matches a step in the Warehouse Picking & Packing Guide. After lesson 7 comes the final test: 10 questions, 8 correct to pass, with an explanation after every answer.
